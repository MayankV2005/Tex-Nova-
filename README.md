Problem Statement

AI & Automation for Manufacturing SMEs

How can automation and AI technologies be applied to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Bhilwara's manufacturing sector to increase productivity?

Many small manufacturing units operate machines continuously without advanced monitoring systems. This often leads to unexpected machine failures, increased downtime, production loss, and high maintenance costs. Since most SMEs cannot afford expensive industrial monitoring systems, they rely on manual inspection, which is often inaccurate and inefficient.

There is a need for an affordable AI-based system that can monitor machine health, predict equipment maintenance needs, and reduce downtime while improving productivity.
